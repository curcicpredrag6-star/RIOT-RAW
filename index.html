MOVEMENT / PEOPLE / NO PRESSURE

Not a race. Not a fitness club. Not another thing to perform at. Just a reason to get outside, move a little, eat something good and meet people you might not have met otherwise.

Run is the beginning. The rest is open.

WELLNESS
WITHOUT THE
WELLNESS CULTURE.

We like moving. We like good food. We like music turned up, long conversations, unfamiliar faces and plans that don't need to end at a set time.

Come for the run. Stay for coffee, brunch, patatas bravas, a playlist, a chat — or whatever happens next. No stats to prove. No finish line to chase.
